App Description

Budget and Passbook is an app bundle that not only keeps track of your checking account, but it also helps save money through it's budget process. When you look at your check book balance, you may or may not have an idea of what that balance is for. By using Passbook you will see what all your money is for.

Features

  • Keeps the track of your checking account
  • Take control of your expenses and plan your financial future
  • Track your bank transactions
  • The app not only lets you track the expenses, but also lets you save it, so you can see and keep a track over time.
  • Stay informed on what assets you own, what liabilities you owe and your financial net worth
  • Start planning for your retirement in a simple, and an engaging way
  • The app not only lets you track the income earned, but also lets you save its details,
