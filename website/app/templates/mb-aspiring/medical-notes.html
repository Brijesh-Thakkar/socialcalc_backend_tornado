App Description

Medical Notes Pro app provides you with a simple, intuitive and spreadsheet like interface to help you add your medical appointments, notes and medical visits.

Features

  • Little or no training required
  • Provides you with monthly calendar
  • Gives you an option to check mark specific dates to prioritise events.
  • Make calendars for medical appointments.
  • Locally save the order as a file, and re-use it with minimal changes.
