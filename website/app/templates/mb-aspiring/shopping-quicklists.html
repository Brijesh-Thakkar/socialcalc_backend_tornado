App Description

Shopping List app helps you to manage, organise and e-mail your shopping lists on the go. The intuitive interface allows you to produce shopping lists in just a minute wherever you may be. Further, the app provides you with a range of list styles to choose from. Simply tap on the style and choose the one you like.This app is specifically useful for people who like to fold the lists up while they're shopping.

Features

  • A range of list styles to choose from. Simply tap on the style and choose the one you like.
  • This app is specifically useful for people who like to fold the lists up while they're shopping.
  • Checkmarks for easy highlighting of important items.
  • Separate section for notes.
  • Simple and easy to use interface so little or no training required.
