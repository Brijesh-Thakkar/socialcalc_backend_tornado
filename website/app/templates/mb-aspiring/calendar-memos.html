App Description

Finding it hard to prioritise activities and track events? 2017 Calendar Memos app provides you with a simple, intuitive and spreadsheet like interface to help you add your important personal, business and medical memos, notes and events

Features

  • Little or no training required
  • View all the twelve months on a single sheet
  • Provides you with monthly calendars
  • Gives you an option to check mark specific dates
  • Make separate calendars for medical appointments, business purposes
  • Locally save the order as a file, and re-use it with minimal changes.
