App Description

Do you feel the need to value your stocks using proven methodologies in a systematic way? Would you like to make better investment decisions and take charge of your portfolio? Do you need to learn how to value stocks while cutting research time by 50% or more? Stock Value app is here to guide you.

Features

  • Help investors to model their investment decisions using proven financial modelling strategies
  • Provides detailed financial statements of unto 5 years
  • Provides detailed financial reports, and also help you visualize the price predicted by both the financial models vs. the current stock price of the company.
