App Description

NetWorth Planner, start planning for your NetWorth in a simple, and an engaging way.

Have you ever felt the need to start planning for your NetWorth? Or, maybe you are approaching NetWorth and would like to do a reality check of your current savings and investments. Whether you're in NetWorth, close to it, or just dreaming about it, the NetWorth Planner helps you see how saving, spending and investment decisions can get you to the NetWorth you want.

Features

  • Visually shows the NetWorth balance against your age starting from your current age to your desired life expectancy
  • Shows how rate of return on your current investment portfolio will affect your NetWorth.
  • You can save and print all your scenarios for future reference
