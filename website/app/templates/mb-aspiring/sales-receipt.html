App Description

Do you need a simple yet professional format for sales receipt? It is customary for every sales activity to be followed by a sales receipt. Sales Receipt app helps you to generate these receipts instantly. You can use the app to issue receipts for both personal and business purposes..

Features

  • Simple interface.
  • Use the basic version for general sales activities
  • Taxes and totals are automatically calculated
  • Check mark the appropriate box to specify the payment mode
  • Save and restore files from Dropbox.
