App Description

Do you need a simple way to track your bank transactions on a savings account? Do you need to maintain a steady account balance and stay within your budget? You may not need costly software to track your savings account. Savings Passbook lets you do it easily, quickly. Most importantly, systematically besides being able to see a running balance as you use your deposit account.

Features

  • Provides a simple way to track your bank transactions on a savings account.
  • Helps in maintaining a steady account balance and stay within your budget.
  • Lets you do all of the above easily and quickly. Most importantly, systematically besides being able to see a running balance as you use your deposit account.
  • The app not only lets you track the bank transactions, but also lets you save it, so you can see and keep a track over time.
