App Description

If you are going on a vacation and want to organise and manage your trip in an efficient manner, vacation planner is an easy to use app for you. Vacation planner app lets you maintain your trip itinerary, and record your flight schedule. You can also keep a list of your contacts along with your packing list. The app also lets you track your expenses and stay on your budget.

Features

  • The itinerary sheets let you track your activities on a date by date basis.
  • The Flight Reservations sheet helps you summarise your flight schedule in a concise way.
  • Stay on your budget, by helping you track your expenses for the vacation.
  • The Vacation Packing Lists let you quickly remember the items needed for the vacation.
  • Simple and easy to use interface, little or no training required
