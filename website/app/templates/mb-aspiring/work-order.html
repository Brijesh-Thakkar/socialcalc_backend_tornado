App Description

Need a simple yet professional solution for creating work orders? Sending out work orders is an essential component of day to day operations of an enterprise. Work Order app provides you with an option to process repair orders, job orders or service orders in a customizable spreadsheet like interface . Simply list the important details about goods and services that you want to obtain from a vendor, a supplier or another department within the same company.

Features

  • Little or no training required
  • Use the Basic Work Order Sheet for smaller jobs or putting together a work estimate or quote
  • Use the Advanced Work Order Sheet for larger, more detailed jobs
  • Capture customer and job information and summarize labor and materials used to complete the jobs
  • Locally save the order as a file, and re-use it with minimal changes.
