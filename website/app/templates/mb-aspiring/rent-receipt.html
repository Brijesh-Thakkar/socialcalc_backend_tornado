App Description

If you are a landlord, or a tenant wanting your landlord to give you a receipt for renting, the Rent Receipt app would come in handy. Rent Receipt app allows you to manage and send rent receipts very easily. The intuitive interface allows you to produce receipts in just a minute wherever you may be. Further, the app provides you with 2 receipt styles to choose from.

Features

  • Allows you to manage and send rent receipts very easily
  • The intuitive interface allows you to produce receipts in just a minute wherever you may be.
  • The app provides you with 2 receipt styles to choose from
  • Simply tap e-mail, to send your rent receipts instantly and get paid faster.
  • You can email the receipts to yourself also.
