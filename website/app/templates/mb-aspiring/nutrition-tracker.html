App Description

An accurate understanding of your food and nutrition is very essential to lead a healthy life. Whether you are on a weight loss programme, have dietary restriction or just a health enthusiast, the Nutrition Tracker app lets you record and summarize your nutritional intake in a convenient and concise manner. The app lets you record up to fifty different food items on four main nutrients at the same time. The information is also shown with the help of graphs, which let you check your nutrition on a daily or weekly basis and also analyse the overall nutritional intake in your diet.

Features

  • Record and summarize your nutritional intake on a weekly basis for an entire month.
  • Keep a track of up to four meals per day.
  • Summarisation with graphs for easy analysis.
  • Track up to four essential nutrients at the same time
  • Simple and easy to use interface. Little or no training required
