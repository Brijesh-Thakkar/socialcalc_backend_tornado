App Description

Do you feel the need to organise your stocks and funds portfolio in a simple and an organised way? Do you find it difficult to keep a track of the loss or gain earned on a particular stock investment? Are you looking forward to be a self-organised investor? The Portfolio Tracker app would be a good starting point for you.

Features

  • Organise your stocks and funds portfolio in a simple and an organised way
  • Record the following details - The stock or fund symbol, The company name or fund name, The date the investment was purchased.
  • The details of the investment including but not limited to the number of purchase shares, the price per share, the last single share value, the change in per-share price, the total market and dollar value for the investment.
  • Intuitive interface. The app requires little or no training to use.
