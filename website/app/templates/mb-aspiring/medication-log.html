App Description

Manage your medications cost effectively in a hassle free manner with the Medication Log.This simple app allows you to track and record your medications over the course of your medication. Comprehensive log sheets have been provided along with the month's calendar and meeting agenda sheets available in two different template formats.The monthly calendar helps you organize your medicines on a daily basis.

Features

  • Track and record your medications.
  • Add and edit details of your medicines like date, dosage times, quantities, etc.
  • Record other details like the due date of the medicine, prescribing physician, etc. as well as details regarding side effects, refill no. and the pharmacy phone number.
  • Monthly calendar helps you organize your medicines on a daily basis
  • Meeting agenda sheets have been provided to bring focus to your meetings with your doctor, physician, etc.
  • Share via email.
