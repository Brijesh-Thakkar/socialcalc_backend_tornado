App Description

Manage your diabetes easily by keeping a track of your blood sugar along with your meals and exercise with the Diabetic Plus application.

Features

  • Little or no training required
  • Track of your health on a daily basis for the entire month
  • Record your blood sugar readings on a daily basis
  • Track and summarise the time spent on the physical activities
  • Plan and organise your meals on a weekly basis
  • Locally save the order as a file, and re-use it with minimal changes.
