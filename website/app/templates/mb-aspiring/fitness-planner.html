App Description

Fitness planner app enables you to keep a track of your exercise routine on a monthly and weekly basis. A meal planner and a monthly calendar sheet of the current month are provided along with the exercise log sheets.

Features

  • Keep a track of your exercise routine on a monthly and weekly basis. A meal planner and a monthly calendar sheet of the current month are provided along with the exercise log sheets.
  • The "Summary" sheet provides a comparative analysis between the goals and the actual time spend per activity on a monthly basis for all the activities.
  • Weekly Meal Planner sheet maintains a log of the meals planned for the week and organize a grocery list for healthy and cost effective spending on food
  • The Monthly Calendar sheet provides a note taking feature to help track the fitness plan on a daily basis for the entire month
